# Abstract (revised for OIR)

> 对应 Q25/Q1/Q19/Q20/Q9：统一 SVW、用 "attenuation"、写明 Light World 与精确数据规模、
> 呈现核心机制（四活动→四价值/五角色→五障碍→attenuation）、删 interactive marketing / metaverse retail
> 与品牌主张；关键词去 young consumers 并加入区分性构念。Emerald 结构式，约 250 词。

**Purpose** – Drawing on service-dominant logic and the value co-creation perspective, this
study examines how user value is collaboratively created and progressively attenuated in a
social virtual world (SVW), focusing on the interplay of interaction activities, co-creator
roles and structural barriers.

**Design/methodology/approach** – A qualitative, abductive design combines immersive
netnography (approximately 100 hours of observation, 110 screenshots, 457 transcribed public
chat logs and an 8,000-word field diary) with ten semi-structured interviews spanning
newcomers, active and highly engaged contributors, and users who had declined or exited. The
empirical context is Light World, a VR-based SVW accessed through PICO headsets. Data were
analysed through abductive, multi-stage coding.

**Findings** – Co-creation emerges through four interaction activities, exploratory, creative,
social and governance, that generate distinct forms of value and configure users into five
fluid co-creator roles along a developmental trajectory, from peripheral experiential
participants to prosumers, organisers and mentors and, for some, to critics who withdraw. Five
structural barriers, spanning access, infrastructure, relational, normative and responsiveness
conditions, form a recursive sequence that gradually erodes co-creation, producing co-creation
attenuation rather than abrupt value co-destruction. Notably, the most committed contributors
may become the platform's most influential critics.

**Originality/value** – The study introduces co-creation attenuation as a processual,
ecosystem-level construct distinct from value co-destruction; extends value co-creation into
embodied, persistent virtual environments; and reconceptualises co-creator roles as an evolving
trajectory within a layered interaction architecture. It contributes to research on online
communities, user participation and the dark side of engagement, and offers role-specific
design and governance guidance.

**Keywords** – Value co-creation; Co-creation attenuation; Social virtual worlds; Online
communities; User participation; Netnography; Platform governance
