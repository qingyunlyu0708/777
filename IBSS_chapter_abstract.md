## Abstract

This chapter investigates the comprehensive transformation of Chinese enterprises through artificial intelligence, moving beyond the common focus on consumer-facing applications to examine AI's profound impact on the entire business value chain. We argue that the strategic deployment of AI across front-, middle-, and back-office operations is creating a new paradigm for operational efficiency, revenue management, and competitive advantage in China's unique digital ecosystem. To illuminate this process, the chapter presents three in-depth case studies, each representing a critical layer of the enterprise.

The first case, situated at the front end, examines how Shizu Group, a convenience-store chain of nearly 4,500 outlets, deployed a vision large-model-based AI attendant system that enables one-click switching between staffed and unstaffed modes. Extending operations to 24 hours, the system fills night-time and temporary service gaps, lifting average daily revenue per store by approximately RMB 1,000 while substantially reducing labour costs.

Moving to the middle end, the second case analyses how a national snack-food retailer rebuilt its 400-store operations on a microservices-based digital middle platform featuring AI-driven intelligent replenishment. The model achieves 99% replenishment timeliness and 90% accuracy, unifies online and offline order fulfilment, and optimises inventory structures to maximise the profitability of perishable, daily-clearance goods.

Finally, the back-end case investigates a dual-mode electronic shelf-label system that integrates ERP, POS, and warehouse systems to automate dynamic, time-slot discount pricing. The solution improves repricing efficiency by 85%, reduces pricing errors to near zero, and lowers operating costs while advancing sustainability goals.

Collectively, these cases provide a holistic view of AI's transformative power. The chapter concludes by integrating these findings to discuss the broader themes of AI-driven competitive advantage in China. This analysis offers academics and practitioners a framework for understanding how Chinese businesses are building robust, AI-centric models that are redefining industry standards.
