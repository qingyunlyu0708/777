## 🅰 最可能被问 · 必须背熟的 5 条

### A1 · "Attenuation 是不是只是 disengagement / decline / fatigue 换了个名字？"
💡 这是**最可能的理论挑战**。答案全在 Table 1 的三个轴。别慌，这正是你准备好的主场。
🗣 "That's the right question to press on, and it's exactly why we built Table 1. Attenuation differs on three axes. **Level of analysis**: fatigue and disengagement are *individual* states; co-destruction is a *dyadic* episode. Attenuation is an *ecosystem-level* property — the erosion of conditions for one role undermines the value others can create. **Temporality**: those concepts are discrete or downstream; attenuation is *cumulative and recursive*. **Mechanism**: it's not resource misintegration in one encounter, it's the *structural withdrawal of the conditions that sustain roles* across the whole journey. So it's not a synonym — it's the connective tissue that the existing, fragmented concepts lack. We position it as an **antecedent** to co-destruction: the slow slope that can end in it."

### A2 · "You're using S-D logic, which is a marketing lens. Where is the IT artifact? Is this really an *IS* contribution?" *(Sarker 的招牌问题)*
💡 **这是他最标志性的关切**（社会技术轴、IT artifact 缺席）。你要把它从"攻击"变成"我们的贡献"。提前在 Slide 5 铺垫过，这里收网。
🗣 "This is central to how we see the paper, and it's why we read S-D logic **sociotechnically** rather than as a pure marketing lens. The IT artifact isn't background here — it's constitutive. The whole attenuation mechanism *begins* in the technical layer: the VR-only access constraint, the instability of the world-building editor, logic failures that make creative labour feel wasted. Those technical conditions then cascade into relational and normative erosion. So our core finding is precisely a **sociotechnical** one — value co-creation and its decline are produced at the entanglement of the social and the technical, not by either alone. If anything, we'd welcome your guidance on foregrounding that entanglement even more strongly in the framing."
*(备选一句，若他继续追 IS 定位):* "We'd argue this speaks directly to the sociotechnical axis of the discipline — the erosion isn't social *or* technical, it's the misalignment *across* the layers."

### A3 · "Your central claim — committed contributors becoming critics — how well is it actually evidenced? Isn't it mostly observation?"
💡 **论文自己承认的最大软肋**（该路径"substantially rests on netnographic observation… triangulated with rather than fully established by interviews"）。**不要遮掩，也不要过度自责**。用"三角验证 + 已观察到的强命题 + 明确的下一步"来框定。
🗣 "You've put your finger on the claim we hold most carefully. It rests on **triangulation**: we observed high-investment users beginning to exit across roughly a hundred hours of fieldwork, and we corroborated that with interviews that deliberately included lapsed and exited users — I10, for instance, is a former high-engagement user. That said, the interview sample alone can't fix the prior engagement level of *every* user who turns critic, so we're careful to present this as a **theoretically salient, observationally supported proposition** rather than a settled causal fact. The natural next step — and one we'd value your view on — is a **role-stratified, longitudinal** design that follows individual users from entry to exit. We'd rather state the claim precisely and flag what would confirm it than overclaim."
💡 这条答完，**主动把它接到你的 Slide 15 第二个 ask**，显得你早想清楚了。

### A4 · "You draw a process/trajectory curve from cross-sectional data. Isn't that inferring longitudinal process from a snapshot?"
💡 方法炸弹。你在 Slide 12 已主动拆引信，这里补足。关键词：abductive、sensitizing concept、analytical generalization、community-level synthesis。
🗣 "Agreed, and we're deliberately disciplined about it. The figure is an **analytical synthesis at the community level**, not a measured individual trajectory — the note on the figure says exactly that. Our logic is **abductive**: we offer co-creation attenuation as a *sensitising concept* and an instance of *analytical* generalisation, not a validated causal model. What lets us speak to process even from cross-sectional data is that we sampled the *full arc* — including people mid-exit — and triangulated observation with retrospective accounts. But you're right that a panel design is what would turn this synthesis into a measured process, and that's explicitly on our future-research agenda."

### A5 · "Ten interviews, one platform, one national culture. What can we really generalise?"
💡 用**分析性泛化 vs 统计性泛化**的区分正面回应，别道歉。这是质性研究的正当立场，Sarker 本人就是这套话语的权威。
🗣 "We're claiming **analytical generalisation to theory, not statistical generalisation to a population**. Light World was chosen as a *revelatory* case precisely because it made the full arc, including decline, observable — which a more 'representative' but still-growing platform wouldn't. The ten interviews are triangulated with about a hundred hours of observation and were sampled to saturation across the engagement spectrum. So the contribution is a *transferable mechanism* — co-creation attenuation — whose boundaries we're explicit about. Testing its transfer across immersive vs non-immersive platforms and across cultures is exactly the comparative work we call for."

## 🅱 很可能被问 · 准备好

### B1 · "Why S-D logic at all? Why not practice theory, affordance theory, or communities of practice?"
💡 承认这些都在场（你确实引了 Lave & Wenger、affordances、Hirschman），说明 S-D logic 是**统摄性**框架而非唯一。
🗣 "S-D logic gives us the overarching vocabulary — value co-created through resource integration — but we integrate several mid-range lenses under it: Lave and Wenger's legitimate peripheral participation for the role trajectory, affordances for the platform side, and Hirschman's exit–voice–loyalty for the decline. S-D logic is the connective frame; the others do the local explanatory work. We found that combination necessary because no single one of them spans creation *and* erosion."

### B2 · "Abductive design, but you use the Gioia method, which is inductive/grounded. Isn't there a tension?"
💡 小问题，但要答得干净。Gioia 是**呈现数据结构**的工具，溯因是**整体逻辑**。
🗣 "We treat the two as operating at different levels. The overall logic is abductive — we move iteratively between data and co-creation theory rather than either pure induction or hypothesis-testing. The Gioia structure is the *device* we use to make the data-to-theory ladder transparent and auditable — first-order codes to second-order themes to aggregate dimensions. So Gioia serves the presentational rigour; abduction describes the reasoning. We're happy to make that relationship more explicit in the methods section."

### B3 · "One researcher did the fieldwork under a single avatar. How do you handle the observer effect and reliability?"
💡 论文已披露并缓解。用反身性 + 三角 + 审计轨迹回答，别声称"消除"了它。
🗣 "We disclose it rather than claim to have eliminated it. The fieldwork was overt where interaction was sustained, and we kept a reflexive field diary explicitly tracking the observer effect of a persistent avatar. Coding was cross-checked independently by a second author and reviewed with the full team, with an audit trail. Observation captured naturally occurring practice; interviews elicited participants' own interpretations — each source checks the other. It's a limitation we manage through reflexivity and triangulation, not one we pretend away."

### B4 · "How would you *operationalise* or *measure* attenuation? Can it be more than a metaphor?"
💡 这其实是善意的"帮你往前推"。给出可测量的**早期指标**（你 Slide 14 已列），把它变成研究议程。
🗣 "Good — and we think it can be made measurable. The construct suggests concrete, observable early markers: creator churn, declining density of co-present users in public worlds, rising rates of griefing/abuse reports, and latency between user reports and platform response. A quantitative operationalisation would track these indicators over time and model how governance interventions bend the curve for different roles. So attenuation isn't only interpretive — it points to a measurable, longitudinal research programme, which we flag as a next step."

### B5 · "What's genuinely *new* about the roles? We already have lurker-to-poster typologies, contributor types, etc."
💡 强调两点新意：**功能架构**（不是活动/动机混合分类）+ **向下轨迹**（既有文献几乎只讲向上）。
🗣 "Two things. First, existing typologies often mix activity, motivation and intensity as classifiers, so the *basis* of the distinction is unclear. We classify by **dominant activity and the characteristic resource integrated**, and treat network position and intensity as descriptive correlates — cleaner and more analytically consistent. Second, and more importantly, existing work almost exclusively theorises the *upward* path from periphery to centre. We add the **downward** movement — central contributors converting into critics and exiting — which is where the attenuation contribution lives. The novelty is the trajectory as a two-directional architecture, not the list of roles."

### B6 · "Is 'map bombing' just the online-toxicity / griefing literature relabelled?"
💡 承认它建立在 griefing 文献上（你引了 Chesney et al. 2009），但你的贡献是把它**放进级联序列**里，而非孤立现象。
🗣 "We build directly on the griefing literature — we define map bombing using Chesney and colleagues. The contribution isn't the phenomenon itself; it's its **position in the sequence**. Toxicity here isn't a standalone cause — it emerges *because* earlier barriers thinned and de-governed the space, and it in turn accelerates relational erosion and exit. We're theorising toxicity as one recursive link in a structural chain, not as an isolated behaviour."

## 🅲 可能被问 · 有个说法就行

### C1 · "Why Online Information Review? Is the fit right?"
🗣 "OIR sits at the intersection of online communities, user participation and platform governance — all central to the paper — and it has published closely related work on sense of virtual community and online peer-production roles (Chang et al.; Fu). Attenuation speaks to that community-sustainability conversation. That said, we'd genuinely welcome your view on whether an IS-core outlet would let us push the sociotechnical theorising harder."
💡 *在 PDW 语境这题也可能来自"要不要投更 IS-core 的期刊"，顺势把球踢回给 Sarker 征询意见。*

### C2 · "Your practical implications — are they specific enough, or generic 'be responsive' advice?"
🗣 "We tried to make them role-specific rather than generic — Table 4 maps each role to concrete measures: cross-device editing and stable test environments for prosumers, scheduling and moderation tools for organisers, visible feedback loops and transparent update logs for critics. The cross-cutting priority is making responsiveness *legible*, because perceived silence is the barrier that closes the recursive loop."

### C3 · "Did you consider the platform operator's / moderator's perspective?"
🗣 "Not directly — our account is deliberately user-centred, which is both a strength and a boundary. Incorporating operator and moderator perspectives is one of our stated future directions; it would let us model the *supply* side of governance responsiveness against the user-side attenuation we document."

### C4 · "The data are in Chinese and paraphrased. Any concern about translation validity?"
🗣 "The authors translated and lightly paraphrased excerpts to preserve meaning while reducing traceability, for ethical reasons in a small community. Coding was done on the original-language material; translation was for presentation. We can add back-translation checks to strengthen this."

### C5 · "You call it a 'social virtual world', not 'metaverse'. Why the careful terminology?"
🗣 "Deliberately. We adopt the established *social virtual world* construct — avatar-mediated, spatial, emergent — because Light World lacks the interoperability and economic infrastructure that fuller 'metaverse' definitions imply. Precise scoping keeps the claims defensible."
