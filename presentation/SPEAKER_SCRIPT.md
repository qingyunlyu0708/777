## 🎯 三条贯穿全程的策略主线（先记住，再看逐页）

1. **社会技术（sociotechnical）是抓住 Sarker 的钩子。** 他最著名的主张就是 IS 研究"丢了技术这条轴"。你这篇的核心机制——*技术层（基础设施）故障 → 级联到社会层（关系、规范）→ 治理失灵*——天然就是社会技术故事。**主动、反复**把它讲成社会技术级联，而不是纯社会/营销故事。这会让他觉得"这研究懂行"。
2. **方法上要稳、要诚实。** 他是 IS 质性方法的权威（2013 MISQ 质性研究指南、2018 JAIS 第一代质性方法）。**主动亮出**溯因(abductive)、Gioia 编码、三角验证、饱和、审计轨迹、反身性(reflexivity)与观察者效应。不要吹，不要藏。诚实地披露"曲线是分析性综合、非纵向测量"反而会加分。
3. **把最亮的发现讲成故事，而不是清单。** "最投入的贡献者→最有杀伤力的批评者"这个反转是全场记忆点，慢下来讲。

## 逐页脚本 / Slide-by-slide

### Slide 1 — Title ⏱ 0:30
🗣 "Thank you very much. My name is Qingyun Lyu, and this is joint work with Jiyao Xun, Zi Ye and Yi Hong. Our paper asks a simple question with a slightly uncomfortable answer: **how does a thriving online community quietly lose the very value its members created?** We study that inside an immersive, avatar-mediated VR social world."
💡 站定、语速放慢、微笑。别急着翻页。第一句话就把"价值先被创造、又被悄悄侵蚀"这个张力抛出来。

### Slide 2 — The puzzle (hook) ⏱ 1:30
🗣 *(读引文，读完停 2 秒)* "This is one of the community's **most invested** users speaking. I want you to notice the whole arc compressed into a single voice — *it was warm… it turned toxic… one last day… and then gone.* That movement, from immersion to exit, is exactly what this paper unpacks."
💡 这是情感钩子。读 I10 的话要有停顿、有语气。**读完那句"never come back"后沉默两秒**再接话——让它落地。

### Slide 3 — The gap ⏱ 1:30
🗣 "Co-creation research is overwhelmingly optimistic: deeper participation, richer value, a climb from periphery to centre. The **negative** side does exist — value co-destruction, the dark side of engagement, fatigue, platform decline — but it's theorised as **separate accidents**, detached from the value-creating process it grows out of. What's missing is an account of how creation and erosion sit on **one and the same trajectory** — and immersive VR, because it's embodied and effortful, *amplifies* both sides."
💡 一句话点明 gap：不是"有没有负面"，而是"正面和负面是不是同一条轨迹"。"amplifies"这个词强调 VR 语境的必要性，别省。

### Slide 4 — Research questions ⏱ 0:45
🗣 "Three linked questions. One: which interaction activities create value, and what forms of value? Two: how do those activities settle users into distinct co-creator roles? Three: how do structural barriers attenuate co-creation across those roles over time? The logic connecting them is **cumulative** — activities generate value, participation crystallises into roles, and barriers withdraw the very conditions that sustain them."
💡 RQ 页是路标，**别逐字念完三个 RQ 的学术长句**，用上面口语版快速带过。重音落在"cumulative"。

### Slide 5 — Theoretical lens *(核心：Sarker 页)* ⏱ 1:20
🗣 "We ground this in service-dominant logic — value is co-created in use, through resource integration. But we read it in two specific ways. First, **compositionally**: not just 'users integrate resources', but *which* resources, through *which* affordances, by *which* actors, generate *which* value. And second — this matters for an IS audience — we read it **sociotechnically**. Co-creation here is **not purely social**. The spatial infrastructure, the world-building editor, the avatar system, real-time voice, the governance tools — these are *constitutive* of the value, not a backdrop to it. Value lives at the meeting point of the social and the technical."
💡 **这一页是给 Sarker 的定制页。** "not purely social… the technical is constitutive"要说得笃定。你在向他证明：你没把 IT artifact 当布景。这一页讲好了，后面的 barrier 级联他会自动接住。

### Slide 6 — Design & site ⏱ 1:00
🗣 "The design is abductive — we iterate between data and theory rather than testing fixed hypotheses. Two data sources, triangulated. The site is Light World, a VR-based social world on PICO headsets: users are avatars, they build persistent worlds, they talk through spatial voice, ten to sixteen people co-present in a world. We chose it as a **revelatory case, not a representative one** — because, unusually, it let us observe the *full arc, including the decline*, in a single setting."
💡 "revelatory, not representative"是你对"样本代表性/泛化"质疑的**先手防御**——主动说出来，等于告诉评委"我知道边界在哪"。

### Slide 7 — Data & analysis *(方法稳健性)* ⏱ 1:20
🗣 "On rigour: roughly a hundred hours of immersive netnography over six months, spread across different days and times; 457 public chat logs, 110 screenshots, an 8,000-word reflexive field diary; and ten semi-structured interviews, deliberately sampled to span the whole spectrum — newcomers, highly engaged contributors, and users who had **already left**. That last group matters: an account of decline needs the people who withdrew. Coding was abductive and Gioia-style — first-order codes, second-order themes, aggregate dimensions — built from the data upward to saturation, independently cross-checked, with an audit trail and reflexivity, including on the observer effect of a persistent avatar."
💡 这页是给方法权威看的"我懂规矩"页。**关键词要咬清楚**：abductive、Gioia、saturation、triangulation、audit trail、reflexivity、observer effect。语速可以稍快但吐字清楚，展示专业度。

### Slide 8 — Finding 1: activities → value ⏱ 1:20
🗣 "Finding one. Four clusters of interaction, each generating a distinct form of value." *(手沿四行往下带)* "Two things worth flagging. Exploration is **generative**, not just consumptive — wandering other people's worlds plants the intention to build your own. And governance activity grows directly out of **relational investment** — as ties deepen, expectations of fairness rise. Hold on to that, because it's precisely why governance later becomes the entry point to decline."
💡 别把四行逐一详述，讲"模式 + 两个洞察"即可。埋一个伏笔："governance 由关系投入长出来 → 后面治理失灵最伤人"。

### Slide 9 — Finding 2: roles as trajectory ⏱ 1:20
🗣 "Repeated participation settles into five co-creator roles, from experiential participant through prosumer, organiser and mentor, to critic. Two moves here. They are **fluid** — the same person passes through several. And they are **functional** — each supplies something the platform needs: attention, content, traffic, onboarding, governance signalling. That lets us reframe roles from a static *typology* into a **layered architecture** — a division of co-creative labour — which connects role research back to the resource-integration premise of S-D logic."
💡 重音："fluid" 和 "functional"。这页的理论增值是"从类型学→功能架构"。说到 architecture 时可以指屏幕上五个角色。

### Slide 10 — The pivot *(全场记忆点)* ⏱ 1:30
🗣 *(放慢)* "This is the most consequential finding, and it's counter-intuitive. The critic is **not** an outside troll. The critic is the *destination* of the trajectory. Many of the people voicing the harshest criticism **began as the community's committed creators and organisers** — and turned only after repeated, unaddressed failures. And precisely *because* they invested the most, they are the most **credible** critics: their disillusioned discourse colours how newcomers see the place and pulls others toward the exit. So engagement is, at the same time, the source of co-created value **and** the source of vulnerability. That qualifies a deep assumption in the engagement literature — that more engagement is uniformly good."
💡 **全场最重要的 90 秒。** 放慢、留白、眼神扫全场。"value AND vulnerability"是核心句。讲完停一下再翻页。

### Slide 11 — Finding 3: barriers (recursive) ⏱ 1:30
🗣 "Finding three: five structural barriers — but the key point is they're **not a checklist, they're a recursive sequence**. VR-only access narrows the creator base at the source. Fragile infrastructure — lag, crashes — discourages the few who do create. Thinning content drives audiences away, so emotional labour turns to exhaustion. Thinned, weakly-governed spaces fill with map-bombing and abuse. And finally platform **silence** converts all of that private frustration into public criticism and exit, which deters the next wave of newcomers. Notice the shape: a **technical** fault cascades into **social** erosion and then **governance** failure. That cumulative, self-reinforcing erosion is what we name **co-creation attenuation**."
💡 再次点社会技术级联（technical→social→governance）。"recursive, not a checklist"要说清。这是把 Slide 5 的框架兑现。

### Slide 12 — The process model ⏱ 1:00
🗣 *(用手沿曲线比划)* "Here it is in one picture. Up the teal slope — participant, prosumer, organiser, mentor — to peak vitality. Then the coral descent, driven by those five barriers, down through critic and negotiator to exit." *(主动补一句)* "One honest caveat: this curve is a **schematic, community-level synthesis** — an analytical model built from cross-sectional data, not a measured longitudinal line. We're deliberately careful about that claim."
💡 **主动披露"曲线是分析性综合、不是纵向测量"**——这是拆掉 Sarker 最可能的方法炸弹的引信。诚实反而显得专业、可信。

### Slide 13 — Theoretical contribution ⏱ 1:30
🗣 "Why introduce a **new** construct rather than relabel an old one? Because attenuation differs on three axes." *(指表)* "**Level** — it's ecosystem-level, not a dyadic actor-to-actor episode. **Temporality** — it's cumulative and recursive, not discrete. **Mechanism** — it's the structural erosion of shared conditions, not resource misintegration in one encounter. It's best understood as an **antecedent** to value co-destruction — a softer, earlier process that can precede it. Three contributions follow: we extend co-creation into embodied VR; we reframe roles as a functional architecture; and, most centrally, we introduce co-creation attenuation itself."
💡 这张表是理论贡献的锚。**Level / Temporality / Mechanism 三个词**是你回答"这不就是 disengagement 换个名字吗"的弹药。说 "antecedent to co-destruction" 时要稳——这是边界界定。

### Slide 14 — Implications ⏱ 1:00
🗣 "If attenuation is recursive, then it's also **interruptible**. For operators: watch the early markers — creators quitting, public spaces emptying, reports going unanswered — and treat decline as manageable, not inevitable. Make responsiveness **visible** — that's the single highest-leverage lever, because unresponsiveness is what closes the loop. Protect the organisers and mentors whose unpaid emotional labour holds the place together. And govern role by role. The managerial question shifts from *how to transact* to **how to sustain co-creation over time** — a continuous, sociotechnical task."
💡 收束到一句可记的话："from how to transact → how to sustain co-creation over time"。再点一次 sociotechnical，形成回响。

### Slide 15 — Close + asks ⏱ 0:50
🗣 "To close in one line: a community does not simply accumulate value, or separately suffer failure — it **builds co-creative vitality, and then loses it along the very trajectory that created it.** I'd genuinely value your guidance on three things: how hard to lean on the sociotechnical framing versus keeping S-D logic in front; how far the committed-to-critic claim can be pushed on this data before it needs a longitudinal design; and where you'd position this for the strongest theoretical contribution. Thank you."
💡 **主动抛出三个问题 = 主动设定讨论议程。** 这三题都是你准备好的主场（见 QA_PREP）。结尾谦逊、真诚，把话筒交给 Sarker。

## 🚫 三个"别做"
1. **别过度推销**（"this is the first study to…" 这类绝对化措辞）——Sarker 会立刻找反例。用 "we extend / we specify / we introduce"。
2. **别在演讲里长篇自曝短处**——局限性点到即止（Slide 12 那一句就够）；深入的自我批评留到 Q&A 用"我们正是这样加强它"的框架说。
3. **别在被追问时变防御**。听到质疑先说 "That's a fair point"，再转成"我们的处理是…/下一步正是…"。详见 QA_PREP。
