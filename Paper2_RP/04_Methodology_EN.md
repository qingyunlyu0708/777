## 4.3 Research Sites, Sampling and Data (the routes)

**Sampling logic.** Section 1 located the study in metaverse brand virtual stores; this section selects sites within that field. Site selection follows the sampling canon of netnography itself rather than that of multiple-case research, so that sampling logic and fieldwork method come from one tradition: Kozinets's criteria of relevance, activity, interactivity, substantiality, heterogeneity and richness of data (Kozinets, 2010, 2020) point to settings where the phenomenon is alive and observable in depth. Because the study's generative engine is sustained first-person immersion, constructs built from thin or dormant sites would be underdetermined by data; sites are therefore chosen for the richness of experience they can yield, with ecological contrast entering as variation across the portfolio rather than as a criterion that overrides richness.

**Eligibility and site audit.** A store enters the candidate pool only if it supports the complete browsing-to-transaction loop, shows content updates or brand activity within the preceding ninety days, offers depth sufficient for a first visit of at least thirty minutes, and is accessible with an ordinary consumer configuration; where platforms publish concurrent visitor counts, a minimum level of visitation applies as a further screen. A two-to-three-week pre-fieldwork audit assesses fifteen to twenty candidate stores against these criteria and is recorded in a standardised table (Appendix A), which also documents failing stores, including abandoned and low-content spaces, preserving the state of the field at the time of study.

**Portfolio composition.** From the eligible pool, four to six stores are selected to maximise variation across the four dimensions of platform ecology specified in Section 2.4, namely economic structure, governance rules, demographic composition and richness of embodiment. An illustrative composition spans a Roblox flagship world of the Nikeland type, a Roblox store using the physical-goods commerce integration introduced in 2025, a Fortnite brand island, a Zepeto brand space and a brand-owned web-based 3D store; the sampling is purposive, aiming at maximum variation for theory building (Patton, 2002). The luxury and mass brand contrast is realised within the portfolio, and brand category is coded as a property of every fragment so that category effects can be separated from ecology effects; where one brand operates in more than one selected ecology, the overlap is exploited for controlled comparison, but the design does not depend on it.

**Inference rules and scale.** Four rules, declared before fieldwork, govern how cross-site patterns are read: a mechanism recurring across sites that differ on an ecology dimension is a candidate general mechanism of embodied commerce; one recurring across brands within an ecology is category-robust there; one confined to a single store becomes a configurational boundary condition, output as a testable proposition; and a systematic contrast between sites differing chiefly on one dimension is read, within the limits of a non-experimental design, as evidence of that dimension's conditioning effect. A platform ecology memo per site, covering platform version, economic structure, demographics and governance, is kept as data. Each store is visited six to ten times for forty-five to ninety minutes over three to four months, yielding an expected corpus of thirty to fifty thousand words of field notes and several hundred codable fragments and friction events, the comparative base required in Section 4.1.

**Data streams.** Six data streams are collected; Table 1 summarises their material and function.

*Table 1. Data streams and their functions*

| Stream | Material | Function |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Reflexive field notes | Written immediately after each visit to the fragment template of Section 4.4 | Core generative data |
| 2. Screen recordings | Full capture of every visit; derived trace indicators: dwell time per touchpoint, retry and hesitation counts, task completion time, teleport and abandonment events | Visual anchoring; fragment sampling; triangulation |
| 3. Momentary self-ratings | One single-item experience and presence score per fragment (Bouchard et al., 2004) | Longitudinal timeline; fragment sampling |
| 4. Public user discourse | Platform reviews and public Reddit and Discord threads; anonymised quotation, no interaction | Resonance check (Section 4.1) |
| 5. Methodological reflexive journal | Researcher identity states, emotional baseline, technical conditions, platform version changes (Section 4.4) | Reflexivity; input to the discussion chapter |
| 6. Consumer validation layer | Four to six adult consumers, metaverse novices and experienced users in balance; natural visits with screen recording and video-cued retrospective interviews (Lahlou, 2011) | Validation and extension of the typology |

The validation layer runs late in the fieldwork, after a preliminary typology has formed: each participant completes one natural visit of forty-five to sixty minutes and a video-cued retrospective interview within twenty-four hours, testing whether the researcher-generated touchpoint types and friction configurations recur in other journeys and capturing frictions outside the researcher's positionality.

**Position of the quantitative embedding.** The behavioural traces and the rating timeline serve two functions only: a fragment sampler selecting deep-description targets by explicit rules (extreme fragments plus theoretical sampling) recorded in the audit trail, and triangulation, where divergence between recorded behaviour and narrated experience (a note reporting fluency while the recording shows four retries) is itself analytic material. This use of analytic metrics follows Villegas (2018) and the quantitizing literature (Sandelowski, Voils & Knafl, 2009; Fetters, Curry & Creswell, 2013); no statistical inference is drawn.

## 4.4 Analytic Rigour Protocol (the map)

On the evocative to analytic spectrum of autoethnographic work, this study is explicitly analytic, and Anderson's (2006) five criteria serve as its quality framework: complete member researcher status; analytic reflexivity; visibility of the researcher in the text; dialogue with data beyond the self, satisfied here through the consumer validation layer and the public discourse archive; and commitment to theoretical analysis. To make the analytic stance auditable rather than merely intended, six discipline devices are built into the fieldwork routine.

First, a dual ledger separates evocative field notes, which are data, emic and permitted to be emotional, from analytic memos, which are etic; the two are kept in physically separate documents and never mixed. Second, every field note follows a structured fragment template with fixed fields for situation, event, immediate emotion, behavioural response and initial interpretation. Third, coding is delayed: at least one week separates the writing of a fragment from its coding, manufacturing analytic distance within a contemporaneous design. Fourth, collaborative interrogation follows the collaborative variant of auto-netnography (Ashman, Patterson & Kozinets, 2021): fieldwork is conducted alone, but supervisors and collaborators act as a theoretically robust sounding board in scheduled devil's advocate meetings, whose minutes enter the audit trail. Fifth, a disconfirming-evidence discipline requires every coding round to include an explicit search for fragments that contradict emerging themes, and the results are processed in memos. Sixth, a methodological reflexive journal, kept apart from the field notes, records researcher identity states, emotional baseline, technical conditions and platform version changes, and is reflected upon systematically in the discussion chapter.

## 4.5 Data Analysis (the learning)

Of the three levels of learning distinguished by Villegas (2018), this study aims principally at theory, that is, at construct and mechanism contributions; learning at the level of brand and organisation is retained as a secondary output, and practitioner-level lessons are collected in the reflexive journal.

Analysis proceeds as abductive thematic analysis in three passes. Open coding marks journey stages, touchpoint forms and friction events. Axial coding links frictions to touchpoints and stages, and codes configurations across touchpoint type, platform resource and experience dimension. Selective coding brings the emerging structure into dialogue with the TCQ and embodiment literatures and with the asymmetry lineage reviewed in Section 2.3.

The codebook enforces the dual-track logic of Section 2.3. Every friction event is first coded on the five antecedent properties, namely intensity, locus, attribution, reparability and design intent, and its journey outcome is coded independently as dwelling, comparing, confirming, rerouting, interruption or abandonment. Good and bad friction are not codes but a mapping to be discovered: which property configurations correspond systematically to constructive outcomes and which to destructive ones is answered by cross-event comparison, so that the distinction drawn by Towers and Towers (2025) functions as a falsifiable hypothesis space, and cases in which the same configuration leads to opposite outcomes in different contexts are compulsorily entered into the disconfirming memos. Promotion and prevention labels are retained as abductive hypothesis tags grounded in regulatory focus theory (Higgins, 1997), without presuming that the firm-level asymmetry reported by Hong and Hu (2025) holds at the level of individual journeys.

Data from the consumer validation layer are coded independently with the same codebook, and both recurrences and novel types are recorded in the audit trail. Cross-site comparison follows the inference rules declared in Section 4.3 and yields output in the form of mechanisms together with boundary-condition propositions. Quality is assessed by narrative coherence, researcher credibility and reader resonance (Ellis, Adams & Bochner, 2011), supported by a complete audit trail.

## 4.6 Writing and Reporting Strategy (the telling)

The findings will be written in a register between the impressionist and realist tales of van Maanen (1988), keeping story and theory in direct conversation, which Villegas (2018) recommends for auto-netnography aimed at journal publication. The findings chapter is organised on the theoretical framework rather than on chronology, following the structural strategy of Ashman, Patterson and Kozinets (2021): journey forms first (RQ1), then the transformation of the nine building blocks and the conceptualisation of embodied touchpoints (RQ2), then the mapping from friction properties to journey outcomes (RQ3). Field-note material enters the text only as data excerpts embedded in this analytic frame, and no part of the paper adopts a memoir register. The double structure announced at the head of this chapter, a study organised as a journey being used to study a consumption journey, is carried through the paper as a connecting thread.

## 4.7 Ethics and Researcher Care (the safety of the trip)

The core data are the researcher's own experiences. Observation is confined to public commercial spaces; no private information about other users is collected; avatars of other users appearing incidentally in screen recordings are blurred before analysis; and public discourse is quoted only in anonymised and non-traceable form, in line with the principle of doing no harm (Ellis, 2007). Because Roblox hosts many under-age users, two additional safeguards apply there: the researcher does not interact with users who may be minors, and no content that could identify a minor is quoted.

The consumer validation layer constitutes research with human participants and will be submitted for ethics review under the university's standard procedure. Only adults are recruited; informed consent covers both screen recording and interview audio; data are stored in encrypted form; and reporting is anonymised. For the researcher's own experiences and the observation of public spaces, the applicable ethical framework will be confirmed with the department before fieldwork begins.

Finally, the design takes seriously Villegas's (2018) element of taking care of the self. Frustration and fatigue during months of immersive fieldwork are simultaneously data and risk: they are monitored through the reflexive journal, and an upper limit is set on the duration of any single fieldwork session.
