# IBSS Chinese Business Casebook 2026/27 — "AI Transformation of Chinese Business"

**Topic: AI-Driven Innovation and Competitive Advantage in Chinese Enterprises**

## Abstract (v1, ~282 words)

This chapter investigates the comprehensive transformation of Chinese enterprises through artificial intelligence, moving beyond the common focus on consumer-facing applications to examine AI's profound impact across the entire business value chain. We argue that the strategic deployment of AI throughout front-, middle-, and back-office operations is creating a new paradigm for operational efficiency, revenue management, and competitive advantage within China's unique digital ecosystem. To illuminate this process, the chapter presents three in-depth case studies, each representing a critical layer of the enterprise.

The first case, situated at the front-end, examines Shizu Group's AI-attended unmanned convenience stores. Powered by visual large-model technology, a hybrid "staffed-plus-unstaffed" mode extends operations to 24 hours across a network of nearly 4,500 stores, cutting labour costs while capturing previously forgone night-time revenue.

Moving to the middle-end, the second case analyses Dmall's integrated AI replenishment and clearance system for daily-clearance fresh merchandise. By replacing manual judgement with algorithmic, store-specific decision-making — "one store, one strategy" — the system reduced Wumart's stock-out rate from 7% to below 2% and cut fresh-food loss to 3%, maximising margins on highly perishable goods.

Finally, the back-end case explores Fresh Legend's time-based discount label. Built on its proprietary Nezha system, production dates are embedded into barcodes so that pricing decays automatically, minute by minute, with a product's remaining freshness — eliminating expired-goods risk, curbing discretionary staff discounting, and converting back-end pricing infrastructure into a powerful marketing asset.

Collectively, these cases provide a holistic view of AI's transformative power. The chapter concludes by integrating these findings to discuss the broader themes of AI-driven competitive advantage in China, offering academics and practitioners a framework for understanding how Chinese businesses are building robust, AI-centric models that are redefining industry standards.
